# Code of conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free project for people of every background and level
of experience. Be respectful, critique ideas rather than people, make room for local knowledge, and
handle public-interest data with care.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, threats, deliberate exposure
of private information, or sustained disruption. Report conduct concerns privately to the project
maintainer. Maintainers may remove content or participation that violates these expectations.
