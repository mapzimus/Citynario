# Resident estimate model card

- **Status:** engineering scaffold; not decision-ready
- **Module:** `resident_estimate`
- **Version:** `0.1.0`
- **Question:** How many residents might occupy the proposed housing units?
- **Formula:** units × occupancy rate × people per occupied unit
- **Outputs:** low, central, and high added residents
- **Evidence:** illustrative assumptions pending Lynn-specific calibration
- **Excluded effects:** unit-type variation, time to occupancy, household formation, and regional feedback

The model exists to prove the contract, trace, and UI flow. Do not cite its current values in a
planning decision.
